Altitudes and the deployment schedule are reported inconsistently across sources, so the film does not state them. In the picture the points fly between 500 and 1,000 km at several inclinations (53°, 43°, 70°, 97.6°, 33°), one point stands for 8 satellites, and one orbit takes ~55 s instead of ~95 min. The ground-sky view is computed for an observer at 51° N, 10° E (Germany); the bright band around 50° latitude is real geometry: orbits inclined at 53° crowd near their turning latitude.

Map data: Natural Earth 1:110m land (public domain), via the world-atlas package; land and sea only, no borders.
