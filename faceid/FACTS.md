# Facts used in the film

| Claim | Value used | Source |
|---|---|---|
| The dot projector casts over 30,000 invisible infrared dots onto the face | 30,000 | Apple, "About Face ID advanced technology" (support.apple.com/HT208108): "projecting and analyzing thousands of invisible dots"; Apple iPhone X keynote / press release (Sept 2017): "more than 30,000 invisible dots" |
| The dots are infrared (invisible); an infrared camera reads the pattern | — | Apple, "About Face ID advanced technology" |
| The pattern's distortion over the face gives a depth map (structured light) | — | Apple Platform Security Guide, "Face ID security": "a depth map of your face" |
| A flat photo has no depth and is rejected | — | Apple Platform Security Guide (anti-spoofing with depth and attention); illustrative |
| The chance a random person can unlock your iPhone with Face ID is 1 in 1,000,000 | 1 : 1,000,000 | Apple, "About Face ID advanced technology": "less than 1 in 1,000,000" (single enrolled appearance) |

The face is a procedural model, not a scan of a real person. The depth values on screen (NASE VOR WANGE, WANGE VOR OHR) are measured on that model at about 104 mm per model unit; they are illustrative, not anatomical averages.
